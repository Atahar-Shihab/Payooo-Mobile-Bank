COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I sign in to the live demo?

Use the demo mobile number 00000000000 and PIN 0000. You can also simply click the Auto-fill badge to populate credentials in one tap, or enter any valid 11-digit Bangladeshi mobile number.

Is real money required to test transactions?

No. Payoo is a frontend portfolio demonstration project. All balances, bank accounts, and transactions are simulated client-side using localStorage.

How do I change the theme or switch to dark mode?

Click the Moon/Sun icon in the topbar or press Alt + T on your keyboard, or use the Command Palette (Ctrl + K).

How do I reset my demo balance and history?

Inside the wallet dashboard, open the Account Profile modal (top right avatar) and click Reset Demo Data, or use the Clear button in the transaction activity feed.

Does the application work on mobile devices and offline?

Yes. The application is a progressive web app (PWA) with offline caching support, mobile bottom navigation, and full responsive touch design.
